Golf's wavetables: the MIT notices and the GPL-3.0 tables

Golf's 130 built-in wavetables (eigen/dsp/gf_tables.h) are, by Golf's own credits (TABELLEN.md, "Bronnen en
naamsvermelding"), Golf's own tables generated from formulas and resyntheses (harmonic levels measured from the source,
rebuilt as new frames) of CC0, The Unlicense, CC BY, MIT and GPL-3.0 sources. TABELLEN.md names the source and the
licence of each; it says the GPL-3.0 text and the MIT notices are "in licences/", a folder Golf's repository (tag
v1.0) does not have. They are gathered here for this module, from the credits TABELLEN.md gives.

GPL-3.0 (Surge XT's factory and third-party wavetables, https://github.com/surge-synthesizer/surge, by Vember Audio /
Claes Johanson and the Surge Synth Team, Venus Theory, The Emu (J Riley Hill), Philippe Favre, A.Liv, TNMG, Ocean
Swift Synthesis, Qonundrai): the tables TABELLEN.md credits to them, converted into Golf's table format. The GPL-3.0's
full text is carried in this module as "GPL-3.0 (the Surge XT wavetables)".

MIT, Mutable Instruments Tides and Plaits wavetable data (https://github.com/pichenettes/eurorack, tides/resources.cc,
plaits/resources.cc): Digital - Drawbar Steps, Harmonic Stairs, Harmonic Steps.

  Copyright Emilie Gillet (emilie.o.gillet@gmail.com).

MIT, the Spherical Wavetable Navigator firmware's wavetable data (https://github.com/4ms/SWN, inc/spheres): Digital -
Folded Ring, Ring Lattice, Spiral Partials. (Its maker asks that its company name is not used on derivatives.)

  Copyright (c) 2017 Dan Green.
