FM1VST @VERSION@

The M-VAVE FM-1 as a plug-in: FM-1 firmwares, each compiled from its own unmodified source, run as loadable cores,
with the FM-1's panel as the editor. macOS 11 or later, Apple silicon.

This installer puts, for every user of this Mac (pick them on the next pages; all three are on by default):

  Desktop app   FM1VST.app          -> /Applications
  VST3          FM1VST.vst3         -> /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  Audio Unit    FM1VST.component    -> /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components

An older FM1VST in those places is replaced. If you copied FM1VST 0.3.0 out of its zip into your own
~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins (the VST3 or Components folder), delete those copies: a DAW would list FM1VST twice.

Then rescan plug-ins in your DAW (Ableton Live: Settings > Plug-Ins > Rescan; Logic Pro: restart it). FM1VST is
under Quixotic7.

FM1VST is GPL-3.0-only (the next page). The licences and credits of everything it is made of (the firmware cores,
JUCE, libusb, the fonts) are in licences/ in the zip on the release page, and the source is
https://github.com/Quixotic7/FM1VST (tag v@VERSION@). FM1VST is independent of and unaffiliated with M-VAVE.
