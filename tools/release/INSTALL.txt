Installing FM1VST @VERSION@ (macOS 11 or later, Apple silicon)
===================================================================

The zip holds three builds of the same instrument. Copy the ones you use:

  FM1VST.vst3        VST3 plug-in (Ableton Live, Bitwig, Reaper, Cubase ..)
                     -> ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
  FM1VST.component   Audio Unit (Logic Pro, GarageBand, Live ..)
                     -> ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
  FM1VST.app         the Standalone app (no DAW needed)
                     -> /Applications (or anywhere)

(~/Library is the Library folder in your home folder. In Finder: Go > Go to Folder, then type the path. Create the
VST3 or Components folder if it is missing. /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/... works too, for every user of the Mac.)


Allowing an unsigned plug-in
----------------------------

This release is not signed with an Apple Developer ID nor notarised, so macOS quarantines what came out of the zip
and refuses to open it ("FM1VST is damaged" or "cannot be opened because the developer cannot be verified"). After
copying, remove the quarantine flag in Terminal:

  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/FM1VST.vst3
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/FM1VST.component
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/FM1VST.app

Or, without Terminal: open the Standalone app once (or load the plug-in in your DAW), let macOS refuse it, then go to
System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll to Security and click "Open Anyway" next to the FM1VST message. For the
app, right-click > Open also works.

Check the download first if you like: shasum -a 256 FM1VST-@VERSION@-macOS-arm64.zip, compared with SHA256SUMS on
the release page.


Making your DAW see it
----------------------

  Ableton Live   Settings > Plug-Ins: turn on "Use VST3 Plug-in System Folders" (and "Use Audio Units"), then
                 press Rescan (hold Option while clicking Rescan for a full rescan). FM1VST appears under
                 Plug-Ins > Quixotic7.
  Logic Pro      Restart Logic: it validates new Audio Units at launch. If FM1VST is listed as failed, open
                 Settings > Plug-in Manager, select FM1VST and click "Reset & Rescan Selection". It is under
                 Instrument > AU Instruments > Quixotic7 > FM1VST.
  Other hosts    rescan plug-ins in the host's settings.

If the Audio Unit still does not show up, run "killall -9 AudioComponentRegistrar" in Terminal (or log out and back
in) and rescan.


Remote FM-1
-----------

FM1VST can use a real M-VAVE FM-1 as its panel. The unit must run the RemoteFM1 firmware:
https://quixotic7.github.io/RemoteFM1/ . Then turn on Remote FM-1 in FM1VST's settings sheet (the corner button).


Your files
----------

FM1VST keeps its data in ~/Library/Application Support/fm1emu/ (presets, themes, extra cores you add to its cores/
folder) and running copies of the cores in ~/Library/Caches/fm1emu/. Uninstalling is deleting the three bundles
and, if you like, those two folders.
